Product information

Additional specifications and fictional usage information for the AeroCharge One portable solar-assisted power bank.

Technical specifications

  • Capacity 20,000 mAh
  • Main charging input USB-C
  • Outputs USB-C + USB-A
  • Status indicator Four-stage LED
  • Housing Impact-resistant polymer

Intended use

AeroCharge One is conceptually designed for students, commuters, travellers and outdoor users who require portable battery storage.

Its solar surface provides supplementary charging in daylight conditions, while USB-C remains the primary charging method for rapid and predictable recharging.
