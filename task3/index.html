Core capabilities

Designed for mobile everyday use

AeroCharge One combines substantial portable battery capacity with supplementary solar charging and multi-device connectivity.

  • 20,000 mAh battery capacity for multiple compatible device charges.
  • USB-C Power Delivery for modern phones, tablets and accessories.
  • Solar-assisted top-up charging for supplementary outdoor energy input.
  • Dual-device charging using USB-C and USB-A connections.
  • Four-stage battery indicator for quick charge-level monitoring.

Built around practical mobility

The solar panel is designed as a supplementary charging source rather than as a replacement for conventional USB-C charging, making the device suitable for travel and emergency top-ups.
